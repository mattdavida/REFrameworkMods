================================================================================
  DMC5 Cheats
================================================================================
In-game cheat menu for Devil May Cry 5. Press F6.

God Mode, Move Fast, More Damage, character gauges, and Red Orbs.
The Cheats tab follows who you are playing (Nero / Dante / Vergil / V).
Docked right. Settings tab can rebind the toggle and redock the window.

This zip is REFramework (dinput8.dll) plus the menu. Unzip into the
game folder — next to the exe.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  INSTALLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Unzip into the Devil May Cry 5 install folder (same place as
DevilMayCry5.exe).

In Steam: right-click Devil May Cry 5 → Manage → Browse local files.

  After you extract it will look like this:

    ...\Devil May Cry 5\dinput8.dll
    ...\Devil May Cry 5\reframework\autorun\simpleCheats.lua
    ...\Devil May Cry 5\reframework\data\re2_fw_config.txt

If you already have REFramework, unzip anyway (or copy only the
reframework folder). The menu script is
reframework\autorun\simpleCheats.lua.

Launch the game, enter a mission, press F6.

Laptop: if F6 does nothing, try Fn+F6.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  USAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  F6
      Toggle the menu (change this on Settings → Keybind).

  Layout
      Shared cheats always show. Nero / Dante / Vergil / V sections
      appear when that character is the one you control.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SHARED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

      Toggles and multipliers save to
      reframework\data\refshell_dmc5.json.

  God Mode
      You take no damage. Health is kept full while this is on.

  Move Fast
      Raises play speed (animation / movement). Off during cutscenes.
      Multiplier on Character: 1x / 1.5x / 2x / 3x / 5x / 10x.
      Default 2x.

  More Damage
      Scales damage you deal to enemies. Your incoming hits are
      unchanged. Multiplier on Character: 1x / 1.5x / 2x / 3x / 5x /
      10x / 99x. Default 2x.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  NERO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Infinite DT
      Keeps Devil Trigger full.

  Infinite Exceed
      Keeps Red Queen Exceed stocks / gauge full.

  Always Color Up
      Blue Rose Color Up stays charged. Shell count stays at the
      unlocked max (1 before Color Up 2, 3 after).

  Mega Buster Keep Charge
      Charge shot stays. Mega Buster is not destroyed. Other breakers
      still break.

  Unlock Super Nero
      Session only. Finale devil body. Does not write the save, so
      the story unlock can still happen. The in-game DT bar may stay
      hidden until the story builds it.

  Devil Breakers
      Adds a breaker the same way a world pickup does: Overture,
      Ragtime, Helter Skelter, Gerbera, Punch Line, Super Buster,
      Rawhide, Tomboy, Mega Buster, Gerbera GP01, Pasta Breaker,
      Sweet Surrender, Banana.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  DANTE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Infinite DT
      Keeps Devil Trigger full.

  Infinite SDT
      Keeps Sin Devil Trigger full.

  Unlock SDT
      Session only. Save stays locked so the story beat still unlocks
      it. Unlocks the form. The SDT bar on the HUD may stay hidden
      until the story builds it.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  VERGIL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Infinite DT
      Keeps Devil Trigger full.

  Infinite SDT
      Keeps Sin Devil Trigger full.

  Infinite Concentration
      Keeps concentration gauge / level full.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  V
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Infinite DT
      Keeps Devil Trigger full.

  Infinite Animal Power
      Keeps Animal Power / concentration full.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  QUICK ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  +10000 Red Orbs
      Adds 10000 Red Orbs to the live count and the save wallet.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SETTINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Window
      Dock Right / Left / Center. Reset Size. Drag the title bar to
      move; resize from the corner.

  Keybind
      Rebind the menu toggle. Default F6.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Enter a mission first. Cheats that need a player do nothing on
    the title screen.

  - Unlock Super Nero and Unlock SDT are session only. They are not
    written to the save.

  - Move Fast is play speed. More Damage is hits on enemies only.

  - Toggles and speed / damage multipliers persist. Super Nero / SDT
    unlocks do not.

  - This is Lua + REFramework. It does not replace game assets.
