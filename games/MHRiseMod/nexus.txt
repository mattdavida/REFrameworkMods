================================================================================
  MH Rise QOL
================================================================================
In-game QOL menu for Monster Hunter Rise (Sunbreak). Press ~ (tilde).

Hunter cheats, health bars, smithy crafts, and wallet adds.
Cheats stay off unless you are solo (no other human hunters).
Health bars and smithy toggles stay on in any session.
Docked right. Settings tab can rebind the toggle and redock the window.

This zip is REFramework (dinput8.dll), the menu, and ref_cursor.dll.
Unzip into the game folder — next to the exe.

If you already have REFramework, copy the lua file and ref_cursor.dll:

  <game-dir>/reframework/autorun/mhrise_qol.lua
  <game-dir>/reframework/plugins/ref_cursor.dll

You do not need csharp-api / REFramework.NET.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  INSTALLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Unzip into the Monster Hunter Rise install folder (same place as
MonsterHunterRise.exe).

In Steam: right-click Monster Hunter Rise → Manage → Browse local files.

  After you extract it will look like this:

    ...\MonsterHunterRise\dinput8.dll
    ...\MonsterHunterRise\reframework\autorun\mhrise_qol.lua
    ...\MonsterHunterRise\reframework\plugins\ref_cursor.dll

If you already have REFramework, you can keep your dinput8.dll and
copy only the autorun lua and plugins\ref_cursor.dll.

Launch the game, load in, press ~ (tilde).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  USAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ~
      Toggle the menu (change this on Settings → Keybind).
      Look input pauses while the menu is open.
      Function keys are used by the game, so the menu is not F6.

  Health Bars hotkey
      Unbound by default. Use the Gameplay toggle, or set a hotkey
      on Settings → Keybind if you want one with the menu closed.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  HUNTER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

      Toggles and multipliers save to
      reframework\data\refshell_mhrise.json.

  God Mode
      You take no damage. Health is kept full while this is on.
      Solo only.

  Always Reserve Health
      Keeps the red reserve bar topped up. Solo only.

  Infinite Items
      Playable pouch and item box do not consume. Town smith is
      Free Smithy Crafts on Gameplay. Solo only.

  Infinite Wirebugs
      Wirebug charges stay full. Solo only.

  Infinite Stamina
      Stamina does not drain. Solo only.

  Always Sharp
      Sharpness stays at max. Solo only.

  Bow Range
      Bow only. Arrows stay in the crit window to 120m. Solo only.

  More Damage
      Scales damage you deal to monsters. Your incoming hits are
      unchanged. Multiplier: 1x / 1.5x / 2x / 3x / 5x / 10x / 99x / 200x.
      Default 2x. Solo only.

  Move Fast
      Raises play speed. Multiplier: 1x / 1.5x / 2x / 3x / 5x / 10x.
      Default 2x. Solo only.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  GIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Armor
      Adds a piece or a full set to the Item Box. Open the box to
      equip. Catalog skips unused / ungendered rows.
      Gift leftovers (no forge recipe) belong here, not the smithy.
      Solo only. Refresh the list after you enter town.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  GAMEPLAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Health Bars
      World-space bars on large monsters. On by default.
      Name, fill, optional HP text, and optional distance.
      Small monsters stay off unless you turn them on — rampages
      will flood otherwise.
      Draw distance and bar scale are on this tab.
      Not a cheat. Works in a party.

  Free Smithy Crafts
      Weapon tree, weapon list, upgrades, forge armor, and layered
      armor. Materials, category pts, and zenny go to 0.
      Works in a party. Do not mash Confirm.

  Unlock All Armor
      Lists every forgeable set on Low / High / Master / Special.
      Gift / arena rows with no cookbook recipe stay hidden —
      those go to Give / the Item Box when earned.
      Switch tabs if a rank looks empty. Works in a party.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  QUICK ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Max Zenny / +10k Zenny
      Adds Zenny through the game wallet.

  Max Pts / +1k Pts
      Same for Kamura points.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SETTINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Window
      Dock Right / Left / Center. Reset Size. Re-docks when you open
      the menu or the game window size changes.

  Keybind
      Rebind the menu toggle (default ~) and an optional Health Bars
      hotkey.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Cheats fail closed if the party list cannot be read. The mod is designed to keep
    off while another human hunter is in the session.

  - Health bars and smithy toggles are not combat cheats.

  - Move Fast is play speed. More Damage is hits on monsters only.

  - Toggles, hotkeys, and multipliers persist.

  - This is Lua + REFramework. It does not replace game assets.
    No extra .NET runtime.

  - Disable any leftover main.lua / healthbars.lua / freetree.lua in
    autorun or the menu stacks.
